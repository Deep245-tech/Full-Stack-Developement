Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup chocolate chips
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. In a bowl, cream together the butter and sugar.
  3. Add in the vanilla and mix well.
  4. In another bowl, combine the flour, baking soda, and salt.
  5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture.
  6. Stir in the chocolate chips.
  7. Drop spoonfuls of dough onto a baking sheet.
  8. Bake for 10-12 minutes or until golden brown.
  9. Let cool before serving.
